About Us

Severse Doner & Pizza is a popular culinary spot in the heart of Kalkan, serving customers until 04:00 AM with a reputation for fresh ingredients and lightning-fast service. Whenever you feel hungry late at night, you can easily place an order via WhatsApp from the beach or your home, accessing Kalkan's most modern QR menu instantly.
